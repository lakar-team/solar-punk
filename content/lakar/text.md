# lakar

Summary of the /lakar page for AIBO context (updated 2026-06-30).

## Lakar Design — Overview

Adam founded Lakar Design Studio in Malaysia in 2012 after returning from his Master of Architecture at Manchester. The core insight: people wanted designer-quality interiors but couldn't justify boutique prices. By treating the design process as a repeatable product — not a bespoke service — Lakar could deliver at 20–30% below boutique rates.

## Company Evolution

- **2012 · Lakar Design Studio** — Pure design consultancy. Clients bring the construction team; Lakar provides drawings, space planning, and concept direction.
- **2015 · Lakar Design & Construction** — One-stop solution: design and physical build under one roof. Added to close the loop on large contracts slowed by approval chains.
- **2021 · Lakar (Malaysia) Sdn Bhd** — Both services consolidated into a limited liability company. Enabled partner structures, clean delegation, and scalability beyond founder-led capacity.

## Key Numbers

- 100% year-on-year revenue growth for 8 consecutive years

- 40+ completed projects across F&B, residential, commercial, and tech-integrated categories

## Project Categories

**Food & Beverage:** Tropical theme restaurant (Sembulan, Kota Kinabalu — adaptive reuse of old standalone building), self-service restaurant (Plaza TTDI, KL), English tea cafe brand (Selangor & KL), food kiosk brand using central kitchen across major malls, restaurant colour scheme update with mural experiments.

**Residential:** Studio apartments (Empire Damansara Soho — early small-space design), bungalow on slope site (500 sqm, hollow blocks), old apartment refurbishments with material experiments, condominium furnishing at affordable price points, link house renovation with partial smart home integration.

**Commercial:** MRT Station KL — 3D modelling service provided before BIM adoption was standard in Malaysia; experiential baby product retail shop (KL); capital investment company office setup (Selangor); outdoor family activity area (Setia Alam, Selangor).

**Tech-Integrated:** Condominium with full smart home (Menjalara), bungalow smart home experiment (Sungai Penchala), water-resistant and fire-resistant kitchen material experiments, bungalow loft conversion and side extension (KL).

## What Made Lakar Different

**Smart Home as a Living Lab:** Smart home integration was field research, not a product add-on. Every protocol (Zigbee, IR, RF, WiFi, Bluetooth) was stress-tested across real client projects before being deployed in Adam's own home. By the time it became a standard Lakar service, every system decision had been proven in use.

**Japan-Inspired Small-Space Design:** Empire Damansara Soho studio apartments were an early experiment in designing for density and liveability before compact living was mainstream in Malaysia. Japan's approach to spatial efficiency became a permanent influence on the practice.

**Material Experimentation:** Sustainable design ran as hands-on research: water-resistant and fire-resistant kitchen surfaces, passive airflow strategies in tropical builds, innovative surface treatments evaluated against real SME budgets and lifespan constraints. Each experiment was deployed in a paying project.

**Early BIM & Infrastructure Adoption:** Before BIM was standard in Malaysia, Lakar provided 3D modelling for MRT Station KL. This included BRT proposals and civic infrastructure visualisation — building expertise before the industry demanded it.

**Affordable Design for SME Clients:** The founding premise — designer quality and boutique prices are falsely linked. High-quality interiors at 20–30% below boutique rates made professional design accessible to SMEs, small F&B operators, and young homeowners that boutique firms systematically ignored.

**Sustainable Cooling Design:** Malaysian tropical climate made passive cooling a recurring design consideration. Early experiments with cross-ventilation, thermal mass in affordable builds, and solar heat gain material choices were direct precursors to Adam's later PhD research in desiccant cooling.

## Design Philosophy

- **Renovation Over New Build:** Malaysia's mass housing stock has hidden opportunity — occupants constantly modify units to express identity. Lakar's model was built on this insight.
- **Design + Technology:** Smart home integration started as a free add-on for architecture clients — a live testing ground.
- **Controlled Growth:** Three entities in nine years, each evolution driven by a specific barrier, not reactive growth.
