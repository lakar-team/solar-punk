# Malaysia-Japan Bridge — Sendai Gas Community Event

**Moon name:** "Malaysia-Japan Bridge" (confirmed)
**Partner:** Sendai Gas (and potentially other Sendai companies with Malaysia business ties)
**Year:** 2025 (inaugural event)
**Status:** Expected to be a yearly recurring event

---

## What happened

The Malaysian community in Sendai had gone dormant for ~4 years during COVID. Adam's public lecture activity (JIA 2024, TAPIO 2025) brought him visibility in Sendai's Japanese-business world. Sendai Gas — a company with Malaysia business connections — learned about him through these talks and reached out to restart relations with the Malaysian community in Sendai.

Adam's approach: Malaysia is always about food. The event became a cooking gathering where Malaysian students in Sendai cooked Malaysian food and presented it alongside food-as-culture context to Sendai's business community.

---

## Format

- Malaysian students in Sendai + some Malaysians working in the area form the core group
- Cook and eat together (Malaysian dishes)
- Present Malaysian food and culture to Japanese business people
- Atmosphere: communal, informal — food as bridge

---

## Why it matters

This is different from the teaching and lecture activities:
- JIA/TAPIO = Adam speaking to Japanese audiences about Malaysia (one-way knowledge transfer)
- MIA schools = Adam educating Japanese children about Malaysia
- Sendai Gas = Adam convening the Malaysian diaspora and Japanese business community together (community diplomacy)

He became the person who could reach both sides. The 4-year COVID gap meant that no one else had stepped into this role — Adam's arrival and visibility as a PhD researcher/speaker made him the natural bridge.
