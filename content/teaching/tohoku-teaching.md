---

## Story angle

A Malaysian teaching English — in Japanese — to Japanese students who need the credits to graduate. The dynamic is unusual: a foreigner who learned Japanese as an adult, now guiding Japanese students through English, bridging both languages.

Unlike a native English speaker imported for conversational practice, Adam brings something specific: he learned English as a second language too, went to university in the UK, and knows what it costs to become genuinely fluent. He understands the student's position.
