# Architecture Talks — JIA Symposium & TAPIO Open University

## JIA — 9th Architecture Salon

- **Event:** 第9回 建築サロン (9th Architecture Salon), Japan Institute of Architects Miyagi Region
- **Title:** "Malaysian Housing Evolution: Then and Now" (マレーシア住宅の変遷：これまでと現在)
- **Date:** August 5, 2024 (Monday), 18:30–20:00
- **Venue:** Oak Tree Ichibancho 4F, JIA Tohoku Branch Office, Aoba Ward, Sendai
- **Audience:** Open to anyone; bring drinks or pay 1,000 yen donation
- **Format:** Architecture salon — professional architects + interested public in an intimate setting
- **Images:** JIA 1–6.JPG (6 event photos in images/architecture-talks/JIA/)
- **Poster:** JIA Talk poster.pdf

Presentation content (6 sections):
1. Early History of Malay Civilisation (Dongsan, Pacific seafarers, delta settlements)
2. Malacca Straits Settlements (Srivijaya → Malacca Empire, Dutch/British colonial, shop-house)
3. Kuala Lumpur Origin (founded 1857, tin mining, Frank Swettenham's rebuilding)
4. Malaysia Mass Housing (kampung → terrace → high-rise; car-dependency, social isolation)
5. Work Experience (S&A Architects, Lakar, PAM Award)
6. Research in Japan (Tohoku PhD, desiccant cooling, Malaysia-Japan architectural dialogue)

Reference cited: Roxana Waterson, "The Living House" (1990)
PDF slides available at: /docs/malaysia-presentation.pdf (already on site)

## Story angle

The same talk given twice, to two very different audiences:
- JIA (Aug 2024): professional architects in a salon setting, discussing housing typology as colleagues
- Tapio (Apr 2025): general Sendai public in a shopping mall atrium, with live interpreters, zero prior knowledge of Malaysia

Both were standing-room-interactive. The Tapio email confirms something remarkable — normally reserved Japanese audiences broke character and asked questions.

Invited as part of a series → suggests reputation building, not a one-off.
