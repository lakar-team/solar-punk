## Story angle

Adam returned to the institution where he was a Dean's Award student — this time as a lecturer. While running a growing renovation firm, he was also in a studio teaching the next generation of architects the same human-centred instincts that were building Lakar's reputation.

Design Thinking and Human Sensibility aren't abstract academic subjects for him — they're the operating principles behind every Lakar project. Teaching forced him to articulate what he'd been doing intuitively in practice. The classroom and the firm fed each other.

200+ students over 3 years. That's not a guest lecture series — that's a sustained commitment to passing something on.
