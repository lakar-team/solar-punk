CONSTRUCTED PROJECT PROTFOLIO
Lakar Design Studio, est 2012
LDS provides design services to clients wanting to develop a construction idea. The construction task is undertaken by the clients own appointed construction team based on the design information provided.
Lakar Design and Construction, est 2015
LDNC provides one stop solution service to clients looking to design and build a construction project. We handle all physical aspects of the project from inception to the requested level of finishing required by the client.
Lakar (Malaysia) Sdn Bhd, est 2021
LAKAR is the combination of the two services under a limited liability company in order to further develop and grow the business model to cater to more market demand.
